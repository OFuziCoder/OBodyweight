[b]Version 1.6.1[/b]

[list]
[*]Improved stability around RaceMenu/SKEE morph initialization. OBW now verifies the BodyMorph interface, the actor and its base before applying a body. If anything required is unavailable, it skips that application safely and writes the exact reason to OBodyNGWeight.log; the plugin remains active.
[*]Fixed the fallback used by Procedural, Procedural Oriented and OBody Sim Weight when SKEE was not ready. It could call OBody's morph-reset routine while OBody's own interface was unavailable. OBW now leaves that NPC untouched until the required interface is valid.
[*]Queued body applications now check that the NPC is still loaded before changing morphs, preventing stale queued work from touching an unloaded actor.
[*]Hardened reroll and preset-export messages for NPCs with no display name.
[/list]

[i]No settings need to be reset. If the log reports that the SKEE BodyMorph interface is unavailable, check that RaceMenu/SKEE is installed and compatible with your Skyrim runtime, then restart the game.[/i]
